# Lighthouse — decisions on record

Fixture content only, invented for Atlas's own test suite. Stands in for the kind of authority
document decision 40 expects under `docs/design/` — the record a workstream's `design` entries
point at.

**1 · Beacon ships in six milestones.** Deep enough that the fixture exercises a tall column in
the depth chart.

**2 · Tide ships in three.** Deliberately shallower than Beacon, so no workstream's numbering is
imposed on another (decision 20).

**3 · Harbor has no milestones yet.** It stays in `designing` until the harbor-master authority is
approved.

**4 · Anchor ships in four milestones, all of them done.** The fixture's one fully-completed
workstream, so the depth chart's head-past-the-last-milestone case has real fixture shape, not
just a hand-built test object.
